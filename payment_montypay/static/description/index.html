MontyPay Payment Gateway for Odoo E-commerce & POS

Accept card payments (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), Apple Pay, and Google Pay through the MontyPay platform

Configuration

  • Navigate to the Website menu and click on Configuration.
  • Click on Payment Providers and open the MontyPay Payment Gateway entry.
  • Enter your MontyPay Merchant Key and Merchant Password.

Checkout

  • Select MontyPay at checkout and click Pay Now.
  • You will be redirected to the secure MontyPay payment interface.

MontyPay Payment Interface

  • Choose a payment method and click Pay Now.
  • On success, you are automatically redirected back to the store.
  • Payment status is confirmed securely via webhook — even if the browser is closed.

Order Confirmation

Payment Transaction

  • Navigate to Website → Configuration → Payment Transactions.
  • View and manage all MontyPay transactions from this screen.

Refund

  • In the payment transaction, click the payment link (e.g. PBNK1/2024/00001).
  • Click the Refund button and enter the amount to refund. Partial refunds are supported.
