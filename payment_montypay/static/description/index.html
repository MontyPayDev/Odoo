MontyPay Payment Gateway for Odoo

Accept secure online payments through MontyPay's hosted checkout — across eCommerce, invoices, and all Odoo payment flows.

Features

  • Hosted, secure checkout page — no card data ever touches your server
  • Works across eCommerce, customer invoices, and all Odoo payment flows
  • Credit and debit card payments — Visa, Mastercard, American Express
  • Apple Pay and Google Pay support
  • Webhook-based payment confirmation (server-to-server callback)
  • Hash signature verification on all callbacks

Configuration

  1. Go to Accounting → Configuration → Payment Providers or Website → Configuration → Payment Providers.
  2. Select MontyPay and enter your Merchant Key and Merchant Password.
  3. Copy the Webhook URL shown in the credentials tab and send it to MontyPay support to configure your callback endpoint.
  4. In the Configuration tab, enable the MontyPay payment method and select a Payment Journal.

External Services

This module connects to MontyPay's hosted checkout service. When a customer initiates a payment, the following data is transmitted:

  • Transaction amount and currency
  • Order reference ID
  • Customer name, email, and billing address
  • Callback URLs and session tokens

The customer is redirected to MontyPay's secure hosted page to enter payment details. No card data is ever stored on your server.

API Endpoint: https://checkout.montypay.com

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get my Merchant Key and Password?
Contact the MontyPay sales team at montypay.com.

Does this module store card data?
No. Customers enter payment details directly on MontyPay's hosted page. No card information is stored on your Odoo server.

What payment methods are supported?
Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay, and Google Pay — all through MontyPay's secure hosted checkout.

Where do I find the webhook URL?
It is displayed in the MontyPay credentials tab inside Odoo. Copy it and provide it to MontyPay to receive payment confirmations.
